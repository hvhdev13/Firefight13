<h1 align="center">Firefight 13</h1>

<p align="center"><b>No holds barred team deathmatch on Space Station 13.</b></p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://discord.gg/XyTBFQRKRk">Discord</a></p>

<hr />

Firefight 13 is a fork of CM-SS13, built on [Space Station 13](https://spacestation13.com) and made in [BYOND](https://www.byond.com). It takes the CM-SS13 model and turns it into a no holds barred team deathmatch game. RP is welcome but not recommended. PvP is mandatory.



## Features

- **Respawns:** die, redeploy, get back in the fight.
- **Loadouts:** pick your gear on a loadout screen before you deploy.
- **Progression:** CoD-style faction, class and weapon levels, each with their own unlocks.
- **Bots:** competent, low pop friendly bots that step aside the moment a player joins.
- **Delimbing:** explosions take limbs. About 99% point blank, 73% at 1 tile, 22% at 2 tiles.



## Arena modes

| Mode | |
|---|---|
| Team Deathmatch | Kill. Respawn. Repeat. |
| Capture the Flag | Kill. Steal. Return. |
| King of the Hill | 10 armed soldiers. 1 hill. |
| Domination | 3 points. Hold them. |
| Elimination *(coming soon)* | 1 life. 7 matches. Counter-Strike style, with de_dust2 and defusal maps to come. |
